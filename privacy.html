Fetch Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 3, 2026

Fetch ("Fetch", "we", "us", or "our") helps customers request shopping and delivery assistance through WhatsApp and other communication channels.

1. Information We Collect

When you use Fetch, we may collect information that you provide directly to us, including your name, phone number, delivery address, shopping requests, order information, and messages sent to Fetch.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to understand and process your shopping requests, coordinate shoppers, arrange deliveries, communicate with you about your order, and improve the Fetch service.

3. WhatsApp

Fetch may use WhatsApp to communicate with customers and process shopping requests. Information sent through WhatsApp may be received, processed, and stored by Fetch for the purposes described in this policy.

4. Sharing of Information

We may share information necessary to fulfill an order with the shopper or service provider handling that order. We do not sell personal information to third parties.

5. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information processed through Fetch. However, no internet-based service can guarantee absolute security.

6. Data Retention

We retain information only for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain business records, resolve disputes, and comply with applicable legal requirements.

7. Your Rights

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your Fetch account, subject to applicable legal requirements.

8. Children's Privacy

Fetch is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any updated version will be published on this page with a revised update date.
