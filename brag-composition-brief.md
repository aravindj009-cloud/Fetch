# Fetch Brag Composition Brief

Create a 20–25 second product launch video from the actual Fetch project on branch `merge-customer-ui`.

## Core idea
People should not have to decide which app to open. They tell Fetch what they need, and Fetch handles the coordination.

## Assets to prioritize
1. Real customer UI from `src/main.jsx` and `src/styles.css`.
2. Real Fetch task/status UI and task-center states.
3. Real product typography and visual language.
4. Real workflow/API behavior where safe to demonstrate.

## Hero interaction
User:
"Get me 2 KitKats and milk"

Fetch:
"Got it. I’ll find those for you."

Then visually compress the workflow into:
UNDERSTANDING → COORDINATING → ACTION → RESULT

## Edit rhythm
0–2 hook
2–5 reveal
5–10 request
10–15 orchestration
15–19 approval
19–23 breadth
23–25 brand punchline

## Visual rules
- Keep UI large and readable.
- Prefer close crops of actual product screens.
- Use smooth camera movement rather than flashy transitions.
- Preserve the off-white / black aesthetic.
- Use restrained accent motion.
- Avoid fabricated provider logos/screens unless they are actually present in the project and clearly framed as integrations/prototype UI.

## Voiceover
"Stop figuring out which app to open.
Just tell Fetch what you need.
Fetch understands the request, coordinates the next step, and keeps you in control.
One conversation. Many tasks.
You ask. Fetch figures out how."
